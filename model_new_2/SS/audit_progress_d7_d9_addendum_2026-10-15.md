# Addendum to `audit_progress_d7_d9_2026-10-15.md` (15 Oct 2026, evening)

## D7: JST house-price source for ITA, ESP, PRT (open item 1)

- The JST documentation file "Documentation - House Price Data" (NPLH_documentation.pdf, linked from macrohistory.net/database) downloads as an image-only PDF with no machine-readable text, so source and start year per country could not be read. Needs a manual look or OCR.
- BIS residential property price sources (bis.org/statistics/pp_sources.pdf) list, for the post-1970 period: Italy = Bank of Italy (based on Consulente Immobiliare) and Istat; Spain = Ministerio de Fomento, INE and Bank of Spain; Portugal = Confidencial Imobiliario and Statistics Portugal. That page gives no start years or dwelling coverage.
- Status of the D0 start years (ITA 1971, ESP 1972, PRT 2005) is therefore unchanged: consistent with post-1970 institutional series, JST source unconfirmed. PRT 2005 matches a private-vendor series (Confidencial Imobiliario) rather than an official one, which is worth flagging as a quality caveat.
