# WDT Research Programme

**26 working papers** · **7 research domains** · Pre-peer review · [Version history →](corpus.html)

New to the WDT? [Welcome](index.html) — the plain-English introduction with the mechanism, the loopholes it closes, and who benefits. This page is the research directory: papers, their questions, and what the project does not yet know.

---

## The argument

The paper domains follow from the proposition. Each domain is necessary, not optional.

| If… | Then… | Domain |
|-----|-------|--------|
| WDT taxes annual changes in net wealth | the change must be measurable | Valuation |
| It taxes gains and refunds losses | the refund must be funded | Revenue |
| Capital accumulates in a public fund | someone must govern it | Governance |
| People respond behaviourally | responses must be understood and designed around | Behavioural |
| This is to become law | a credible transition path must exist | Implementation |

---

## If you have 1–2 hours — the core sequence

These five papers establish the full architecture. Reading them in order gives a complete picture of mechanism, foundations, valuation, governance, and fiscal viability.

1. White Paper (WP) — mechanism design, principal objections, implementation pathway
2. Moral Foundations (MF) — why the delta base, and the cooperative architecture
3. Valuing Wealth (VAL) — how the four valuation routes work
4. Constitutional Governance (GOV) — how the SWF is governed and protected from capture
5. Rates and Revenue (RATES) — what the WDT raises and under what assumptions

## What the project does not yet know

These are questions the papers have identified as unresolved. They are stated here because intellectual honesty requires it, not despite it.

- The formal welfare comparison between a delta base and a consumption tax has not been completed. → (LR.A §2.2)
- The Domar-Musgrave extension to a progressive delta-based wealth tax has not been formally modelled. → (LR.A §2.1)
- The cross-base fiscal externality of migration has not been incorporated into any behavioural revenue model. → (LR.A §3.2)
- Optimal rate design for the progressive delta schedule under behavioural responses has not been solved. → (RATES)
- The valuation protocol for illiquid partnership interests and carried interest has not been finalised. → (VAL)
- The constitutional entrenchment mechanisms for the SWF have not been stress-tested against UK parliamentary sovereignty doctrine. → (GOV)
- The interaction between WDT and existing pension tax relief structures has not been modelled. [→ SCOPE](scope.html)
- The minimum fund capitalisation required to maintain refund credibility through a 2008-scale crash has not been formally derived. → (RATES)

The complete register of open questions across all papers is in the [Project Map](Project-Map.html). Version history and paper status are on the [Papers index](corpus.html).
