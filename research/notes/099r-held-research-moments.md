# 099r: moments from the research already held

Status: extraction note for phase 2 of the household-flow refactor (`docs/model-v3/household-flow-plan.md`, Scenario B). It records moments only; it chooses no parameter and edits no register row. Date 2026-11-21. Machine-readable copy: `research/tables/099r-held-research-moments/moments.csv` (same columns as the table in section 3).

## 1. Method and conventions

- All 18 PDFs in `literature/` and the ETB xlsx in `data/uk-source-workbooks/` were opened with `pdftotext -layout` (pages split on form feed) and every number below was checked against the page text. Chart bar labels that the text layer scrambles were read from the page image (Before the fall Fig 7 p22, Fig 22 p47; Advani, Bangham and Leslie Fig 6 p15). No PDF was unreadable, so none is logged as unreadable.
- Page numbers are PDF pages. Printed or journal pages: Before the fall, same; Advani and Tarrant, journal = PDF + 508; Summers, journal = PDF + 404; Advani, Bangham and Leslie, PDF 14 = journal 409; Tippet and Zucman, printed = PDF - 1; Tax by Design, book = PDF - 14; IFS Green Budget, printed = PDF - 1.
- Stage means self-reported retired (098r). Nothing read here splits the retired from the economically inactive who are not retired, so every "stage" cell says none, an age proxy, or the source's own definition.
- Band schemes in these sources are family or per-adult wealth deciles drawn in their own sample, wealth quintiles, income groups, or fixed pound thresholds in the extreme tail. None is the pack's household decile band or a WAS household pound band. By the 098r `was_band_edge_mapping.csv` (cumulative household shares 12, 28, 42, 52, 67, 87%), the WAS pound bands fit the pack's bands only roughly; per-adult family deciles from the Resolution Foundation do not fit them at all without microdata.
- Pension accrual: Before the fall excludes DB pensions and pensions in payment from the passive and active split but includes DC pension saving and gains in the total change. The ONS saving ratio is roughly half pension accrual (097r s9). The Green Budget ratio is that total ratio.
- A moment is not turned into a saving or depletion rate where it does not measure one. "Active" in the Resolution Foundation split is the residual of saving, debt change and transfers.

## 2. Per file

### 2.1 Before the fall (Resolution Foundation, 61 pp)
Measures how the 2008 to 2022 rise in GB wealth split into active accumulation and passive asset-price gains, and how families moved across the wealth distribution. Population: family wealth per adult (benefit unit) from WAS, GB; two-year changes between rounds 2008-10 to 2020-22; mobility for people aged 25-54 in 2016-18. Wealth = WAS total net wealth on the RF's own DB pension valuation (market annuity rates, not ONS SCAPE), so its total (17tn) differs from the ONS-basis 13,568bn held in `data/rates/totalwealthtables.xlsx`. Saving is never reported as a rate: active change is the residual of passive (class returns on opening holdings). Returns are two-year asset-price returns by quintile, shown in a chart only (Fig 9). Usable numbers: BF-01 to BF-15.

### 2.2 Advani, Bangham and Leslie 2021 (Fiscal Studies 42)
UK wealth distribution and the characteristics of high-wealth families, WAS 2016-18. Wealth = family net wealth including pension, business and physical wealth. Usable: the financial-wealth passive share (AB-01), top-decile persistence (AB-02), a median-household accumulation illustration (AB-03), financial wealth growth (AB-04) and the zero-return share of financial wealth by decile (AB-05). Its WAS-missing-tail adjustment (about 5% of wealth, top 1% 18% to 23%) is already in the register as I-D4d and I-D4e and is not recorded again.

### 2.3 Advani and Tarrant 2021 (Fiscal Studies 42), behavioural responses to a wealth tax
A review of elasticity and avoidance evidence from other wealth taxes. Population: mostly foreign; no UK band-level data. Returns appear only as assumed values for arithmetic (AT-01). It concludes minimal evidence of reduced saving (PDF 2). Usable: AT-01 to AT-03, all context only.

### 2.4 Summers 2023 (OxREP), effective tax rates at the top
HMRC tax records of UK individuals above 100,000 pounds income or remuneration, tax years 2007-08 to 2017-18; remuneration = taxable income plus realised gains. Accrued gains are omitted and noted as a limit (PDF 4, 6). Usable: SM-01, SM-02. Ranked by remuneration, not wealth.

### 2.5 Tippet and Zucman 2026 (ITO Working Paper 46)
Revenue estimate for a 2% minimum tax above 100m pounds. Wealth = Rich List net worth plus a Pareto fit below the list. Usable: the UK top-tail band table (TZ-01) and the top-tail aggregate growth (TZ-02).

### 2.6 EER 2025, Measuring top wealth shares in the UK (Advani, Summers, Tarrant)
Top 1% share of total wealth 2016-18 is 14.4% on survey aggregates and 16.5% on national-accounts aggregates (PDF 2, 3, 13); 12% to 18% over 2006 to 2018 (PDF 13). It is the source of the register's low top-1% share I-D4d (14.4); the central 18.0 is WAS as published and the high 23.0 the missing-tail adjustment, so the three are different constructions. It gives no saving, return or gains split of those shares, and no band-level share by component. Recorded as a share comparison in the table below (EER-01).

### 2.7 Zucman 2024, G20 report
Global minimum tax proposal for billionaires. Usable: the decomposition of billionaire wealth growth (7.1% real, 1987 to 2024) into return and consumption (ZM-01). Not UK.

### 2.8 Tax by Design (Adam et al. 2011) ch 13 and 15
Tax-design chapters. Usable: ELSA 2004 financial wealth composition by decile (TD-01) and estates at death 2007-08 (TD-02). Both context only.

### 2.9 IFS Green Budget 2025 (346 pp)
Chapter 1 gives the household saving rate and a forecast (GB-01, GB-02). Fig 1.2 (real liquid deposits against pre-pandemic trend, PDF 17) is a chart without values and shows excess savings now in deficit; recorded in words only. The rest of the report is fiscal; no wealth-band or stage moment.

### 2.10 Effects of taxes and benefits FYE 2024 (ONS bulletin PDF) and etbreferencetablesfye202324final.xlsx
Income, taxes and benefits by decile for retired and non-retired households. The numbers are already held in `data/uk-wealth-tables/098r/etb_income_by_decile_retired_nonretired.csv` and are not extracted again. The PDF supplies the stage definition (PDF 11): a retired household is one where more than 50% of income comes from retired people; a retired person is self-defined retired and over 50 (the other criteria are in the PDF). This is household-based and age-limited, so it is not the self-reported-retired stage used in the pack (098r).

### 2.11 Bozio, Emmerson, O'Dea and Tetlow 2013
Read and extracted in `research/notes/095r-bozio-2013-reading.md`; not redone here.

## 5. Advani, Summers and Tippet-Zucman on the saving, returns and capital-gains component of top-tail wealth

- **Advani and Tarrant 2021.** Gives no UK measure of the saving, returns or capital-gains share of top wealth. Returns appear only as assumed 4%, 4.5% and 3% for arithmetic (AT-01). Saving evidence is from other countries (AT-03) and it reports minimal evidence of reduced saving under a wealth tax (PDF 2). No UK band-level share.
- **Summers 2023.** Realised gains only, by remuneration rank, for individuals above 100,000 pounds income. More than 90% of taxable gains go to that group; the top 1% receive an average 47,000 pounds of gains beside income (SM-01). Accrued gains are said to be omitted (PDF 4, 6). The only accrued-gains figure (US top 25, 3.4%, PDF 2) is not UK. No wealth-band share; a remuneration rank is not a wealth band.
- **Tippet and Zucman 2026.** Gives a UK top-tail band table (TZ-01, thresholds 50m to 1,000m pounds, 129 to 1,710 households) and a growth rate of the top Rich List aggregate (TZ-02, 6.48% a year, nominal). The growth rate mixes price change, saving, consumption, transfers and entry and exit and is not split; it is not a return or saving rate. The table is a UK band-level count and wealth but not a share by saving, returns or capital gains. The one extra component source is Zucman 2024 (ZM-01, global).
- **Answer to "any UK band-level share of the component split":** none in the three papers.
