# Model v3: hub problem outputs (brief 014g, draft for the guru's merge)

Defines OH1a to OH1c and the data facts D-H1a to D-H1c for hub section H1 (the problem). Proposals only: nothing here is in `metrics.md`, `outputs.yml` or `inputs.md` yet; the guru merges after 014g to 016g are done. Author's status (2026-11-17): accepted "for now, will refine later".

Ids. Metric ids here are provisional (`MH1a` to `MH1c`) so they cannot collide with the ids 015g and 016g propose; the guru renumbers at the merge. Output ids are the ones the author accepted.

Reading rule. Today's-system quantities (OH1b, OH1c) do not depend on the WDT schedule, so they are reported once, not per named schedule. OH1a depends on the schedule, so it is reported for S_low, S_central and S_high. Money and bands follow `metrics.md` (real, wealth bands as in O2, G = 30, Green Book discounting from I-D11a to I-D11c).

## 1. OH1a: concentration path with and without the WDT
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Metric row (MH1a) | **Top-q share of adult net wealth.** MH1a(q, t, s) = net wealth held by the top q of adults, ranked by net wealth, divided by the net wealth of all adults, at the start of year t under scenario s. q = 1% and 10%. s0 = today's taxes continue (nothing retired, no WDT); s1 = WDT at a named schedule with the six taxes retired by the stated order and trigger (M1e). Output = MH1a(1%, t, s0), MH1a(1%, t, s1) and the difference in percentage points, at t = 10, 25 and 50. Denominator: total net wealth of all adults, payers and non-payers (one denominator; D-H1a is the t = 0 value and history of this same metric, not a second definition). Unit: % of total adult net wealth; difference in percentage points. Horizon: years 10, 25 and 50 on the path (none is read as a steady state). Summary: p10_mean_p90 over return paths (level 1), per named schedule |
| Inputs | I-D4d (top-1% share, 2016-18, old pension basis: starting point, flagged), I-D4b and I-D4c (payers' share of adults and of wealth), I-D4e and I-D4f (tail add-on and Rich List), I-D1a to I-D1d (average return and loss years). **MISSING 1:** return by wealth rank, the gap between the top 1% and the median holder's return (the "Fagereng premise" in the ledger; the register's D1 rows give one return for everyone). **MISSING 2:** saving and consumption by wealth band, and mobility into and out of the top 1% (inheritance, entry). **MISSING 3:** the top-1% and top-10% shares on the Round 8 per-adult basis (I-D4d is 2016-18 and another basis) |
| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: OH1a, metric: MH1a, title: "Concentration path: top 1% share with and without the WDT", summary: p10_mean_p90, by: [q_1pct, q_10pct, year_10_25_50, scenario_s0_s1], element: [H1, S6], kind: problem}` |
| Claim it lets a spoke make | "Without a change, the top 1% hold X% of wealth at year 50 (P10 to P90); with the WDT, Y%." Conditional: the rise comes from MISSING 1, so a hub sentence must say so |

Flags. (a) The output is only as strong as the return gradient: set it to zero and the s0 path is flat. State the gradient and its source beside the number, and treat "concentration rises" as a premise with a stated source, not a finding of the model. (b) It is the same output the ledger lists as the concentration output for S6 (`OW3` in 015g): 014g owns the definition; 015g refers to MH1a and does not define a second one. (c) For stage C: a representative-agent state cannot produce it. v3 needs a wealth distribution that evolves by rank, which sizes the core; if that is too costly, OH1a is the output to drop.

## 2. OH1b: rate on gains for large holders today, against a worker's tax rate
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Metric rows | **M3 on today's system (a view of M3, no new metric).** M3 with the tax system set to today: PV of CGT and IHT paid over a payer's life divided by PV of the gains the payer accrues over that life. Same denominator as M3 (PV of accrued gains), same G = 30, same discount schedule. Like-for-like with M3 because CGT and IHT are the taxes the WDT retires; income tax stays in both. **MH1b, worker's average tax rate:** (income tax + employee NICs) divided by gross earnings, at median full-time earnings, current rates and thresholds. Denominator: gross earnings. Unit: %. Horizon: one year (the rate schedule of the data pack year). Summary: M3-today p10_mean_p90 (overall and by wealth band, for payers); MH1b a point |
| Second line to consider | **M3-all-tax:** the same, with income tax on investment income added to the numerator (same denominator). This is the like-for-like comparison with a worker's income tax; without it the hub overstates the gap. Recommended as the line the H1 claim rests on, with M3-today beside it |
| Inputs | I-M6h, I-M6i (CGT over wealth: payers, D10), I-M6k (CGT £bn), I-M6j (share from gains of £5m or more), I-M6a to I-M6g (IHT over wealth, effective rates by estate size), I-M6l (income tax effective rate, £1m or more), I-D1a (accrued gain per £ of wealth), I-D11a (discount rate). **MISSING 4:** the worker's income tax and NICs: current bands, rates, personal allowance, the median full-time earnings figure (ONS ASHE). **MISSING 5:** investment income by wealth band and the income tax on it (for M3-all-tax). **MISSING 6:** accrued gains by wealth band for the denominator; I-D1a gives a mean return, not a band profile |
| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: OH1b, metric: M3, system: today, title: "Lifetime rate on gains today (payers) against a median worker's income tax and NICs", summary: p10_mean_p90, by: [overall, wealth_band], comparator: MH1b, element: [H1], kind: problem}` and, for the comparator, `- {id: OH1b2, metric: MH1b, title: "Median worker's average income tax and NICs rate", summary: point, element: [H1], kind: problem}` |
| Claim it lets a spoke make | "Large holders pay X% of their gains in the taxes the WDT replaces; a median worker pays Y% of earnings." (A rough check from the register: CGT 0.144% and IHT 0.056% of wealth a year against a 4.42% mean return gives about 4.5%, my arithmetic only.) |

Flags. (a) M3's word "gains" must be one thing. I-D1a is a total real return (income plus capital gain) and the WDT's delta is the change in net wealth; if M3 means capital gains only, the denominator in OH1b changes by the income share. Recommend M3 = real change in net wealth, and say so in the merge. (b) IHT falls on estates, not on gains: including it in a rate on gains is a convention, stated beside the number.

## 3. OH1c: share of lifetime gains that escape tax today
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Metric row (MH1c) | **Escape share.** MH1c = PV of the gains that are never charged to CGT over a payer's life, divided by PV of gains accrued over that life, by wealth band. Never charged: unrealised at death and uplifted, held in exempt wrappers (main home, ISAs, pensions), inside the annual exempt amount, or relieved. Denominator: PV of accrued gains (same as M3; the discounted share, so deferral enters through the discounting, not a second definition; the undiscounted share is not reported). Unit: % of PV of gains. Horizon: one life, G = 30. Summary: p10_mean_p90 over return paths and realisation draws |
| Inputs | I-M8b (static revenue from ending the CGT uplift at death: a cross-check on the uplifted share), I-M6j and I-M6k (largest gains and total CGT), I-M6h and I-M6i (CGT over wealth). **MISSING 7:** holding period and realisation rate by wealth band (the ledger's 012r input). **MISSING 8:** the share of accrued gains in each exempt class (main home, ISAs, pensions, business relief) by band, which decides how much of the escape is law and how much is deferral. **MISSING 6:** accrued gains by band, as above |
| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: OH1c, metric: MH1c, title: "Share of lifetime gains that escape tax today", summary: p10_mean_p90, by: [wealth_band], element: [H1], kind: problem}` |
| Claim it lets a spoke make | "X% of the gains on large wealth are never taxed today (P10 to P90), Y% of that through the uplift at death" |

Flag. Report the escape in two lines, by cause: deferral then uplift at death, and exempt by law. The hub's argument is about the first (a design gap), the second is a choice of Parliament; one total would blur them.

## 4. D-H1a: wealth concentration facts, with a history
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Fact row | **Top-1% and top-10% share of adult net wealth, and wealth to GDP, now and over time.** The shares are MH1a at t = 0 (same definition, same denominator), plus a history. Wealth to GDP is I-D4a's ratio (household wealth, Round 8, nominal GDP of the wave midpoint). Unit: % and a ratio. Summary: point with the I-D4a, I-D4d range; history as a series |
| Inputs | I-D4a, I-D4d, I-D4e, I-D4f, I-D4g. **MISSING 3:** the shares on one per-adult basis. **MISSING 9:** a time series of the top-1% and top-10% wealth shares, UK (the WID extract is held under `model_v2/wid_out/`, not yet a register row) |
| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: DH1a, title: "UK wealth shares and wealth to GDP", summary: point_and_range, element: [H1], kind: data_fact}` |
| Claim | "The top 1% hold X% and the top 10% Y% of adult wealth; household wealth is Z times GDP" |

Flags. (a) The ledger's claim 21 reads "top 10% about half". The register shows 51% (Advani, Bangham and Leslie, 2016-18, old pension basis) and 40.7% (Round 8 deciles, households, new basis), so "about half" holds on one basis only. D-H1a must state one basis. (b) Wealth to GDP is 5.8 to 7.5 on the survey (I-D4a) and 3.6 on the national balance sheet (I-D4g): two numbers for one label. Recommend the hub states I-D4a, because it is the base the tax would measure, and names I-D4g as the national-accounts cross-check.

## 5. D-H1b: the tax mix
| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Fact row | **Receipts by head as % of GDP and % of total receipts, grouped into labour, consumption, and capital and wealth.** Source: the held IFS sheet, "% GDP (cash)", 2025-26. The grouping is a stated rule, so the hub's "leans on labour" is a count, not a judgement: labour = PAYE and self-assessment income tax on earnings and employee and employer NICs; consumption = VAT and duties; capital and wealth = corporation tax, CGT, IHT, stamp duties, council tax, business rates. Unit: % of GDP. Summary: point |
| Inputs | I-D7b (corporation tax, 3.37). **MISSING 10:** one row per remaining head from the held sheet (the register has corporation tax only; the six retired taxes' lines and income tax, NICs, VAT are not rows). The retired heads must read the same cells as M1e (8.27% total), so the hook and M1e cite one source, not two copies |
| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: DH1b, title: "UK tax mix by head and by group", summary: point, element: [H1], kind: data_fact}` |
| Claim | "X% of receipts come from labour, Y% from capital and wealth" |

Flag. The sheet does not split income tax into earnings and investment income. The labour group is therefore an upper bound; say so beside the number, or request the HMRC split (MISSING 5 covers part of it).

|---|---|

| `outputs.yml` line | `- {id: DH1c, title: "Tax bill of non-payers by income decile", summary: point_and_range, by: [income_decile], element: [H1, S6], kind: data_fact}` |

Flag, for the guru. M4a says "by income decile of the non-payers". This file uses the second, because it is publishable from S23; the guru should confirm or amend M4a at the merge, since M4a and D-H1c must share one decile definition.
