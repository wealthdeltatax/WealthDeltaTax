## Gaps and conflicts
- **No UK series by wealth band** for investment income, accrued gains, realisation or exempt shares. Rows use Round 8 decile aggregates with stated judgement or income-band proxies.
- **CGT conflict.** The held IFS sheet shows CGT £19.5bn for 2024-25 and £21.2bn for 2025-26 (OBR March 2023 forecast vintage); HMRC reports liabilities of £24.2bn for 2024-25. Receipts and liabilities differ in basis and vintage.
- **HMRC share of gains to high incomes:** 52% in the summary and 55% in the detailed commentary of the same release (I-T5d).
- **Round 8 is GB; HMRC, ISA and IFS figures are UK.** Ratios mix them (marked in each row).
- **Main home, ISA and business relief shares by wealth band** are not in the held tables or the pages read. The HMRC ISA total (£952bn) is the only ISA figure.
- **Uplift at death** primary text not read (gov.uk inheritance page off the allowlist); the CAGE briefing states it.
- **Dividend and savings detail** (£500 allowance, savings starting rate, personal savings allowance) not re-read.
- **Not fetched:** the HMRC income-tax liabilities tables by income range and ONS adhoc wealth-by-decile files (data sheets, not read through the fetch tool). Investment income by decile is the likely route for a later pass.
