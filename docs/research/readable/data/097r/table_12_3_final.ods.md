| These tables contain Accredited Official Statistics for the composition of estates notified to HMRC using Inheritance Tax returns. The values are shown before the deduction of reliefs and exemptions outlined in Table 12.2. These tables also provide a breakdown of estates with and without a tax liability, by the size of tax liability and composition of their estate. The data are rounded to three significant figures. |
