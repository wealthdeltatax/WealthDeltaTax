## Goal
The old papers and site pages no longer present a population centre near alpha 1.1, or "mild overstatement" as the stable or rational equilibrium, as a finding. The tolerant zone (alpha about 0.8 to 1.5), the asymmetry of understatement and the declaration curve are model results and stay.

## Decisions (author, 2026-11-20)
- Remove the claim. Evidence (080r): the centre near 1.1 is model-implied from the refund asymmetry; no source measures or tests it; a centre above 1 also arises from owners overestimating their homes, with no refund motive. The evidence that exists (Sweden, Colombia) shows understatement where no third party reports value, with a truthful minority of roughly one in five.
- Do not replace it with a different number. Where the text needs a sentence, say the model's tolerant zone is a result of the model, and that no study measures how payers actually declare.
