| `[rates.dist]` cells and `adults_m` unit | Whether the wealth distribution is per household or per adult | Not recorded | **Checked against Advani, Bangham and Leslie (CAGE WP 576, 2021), 15 Oct.** Their headline results use families (a single adult or a couple, with any dependent children), not households or individuals: "we rely on wealth per adult within family units". Thresholds are per adult within the family; decile shares (the 51% top-10% and 18% top-1% survey shares in the TOML) are shares of total net family wealth, with individual and household versions in appendices. So the TOML shares rank families but the cells are labelled top fractions of adults. Because couples count twice among adults, the top X% of adults is not the top X% of families, a small bias that grows with the share of couples in the top. Within a couple the family basis already equal-splits wealth. The paper's £14.4tn WAS total and 5% Rich List uplift match the TOML (`survey_total_bn`, `extra_central_frac`). | **Verified: family basis, not adult basis** | Re-derive cell shares from the appendix individual version, or reweight family shares to adults; flag until done. |
| `wmin_house_ratio` = 1.0 (W_min per adult) | Threshold at 1.0 times the median house price | Judgment | **Decided 15 Oct (user): W_min is per person.** With household data (equal split), the per-adult threshold is half the median house price, so a couple owning one median-priced house sits at the threshold. Required TOML change: `wmin_house_ratio` 1.0 to 0.5, by numbered patch with a test (not made here). Notes: 0.5 assumes two adults per owner household; single-adult owners push the right ratio towards 1 (EHS 2013-14: 29% of outright owners are a single person aged 60 or over), so the ratio is approximate and worth a sensitivity. The rationale to record is definitional (an adult's equal share of a median house), not the revenue effect, which should be reported as an outcome (spec principle 1). `house_median_gbp_m` (0.225m, 2017) is still a placeholder. | **Decided; TOML patch pending** | Patch the TOML; add a ratio sensitivity (0.5 to 1.0); source the median house price. |
