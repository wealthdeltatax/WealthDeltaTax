### 3. Technical findings
- **`target_not_met` and the cap.** `core/support.py` `solve_rate` bisects the comparator rate on [0, 1]. Without a cap it raises `ValueError` when a rate of 1.0 raises less than the target. Inside `with capping(store):` it returns 1.0 and does `store.setdefault(kind, (reached, target))`, so the first shortfall, level 1's, stands. Outside the block (all other callers, all unit tests) it still raises. `run_point` wraps level 1, the tornado and O1e in one block. First attempt wrapped level 1 only: the same error then came from the tornado's evaluations (outside the block), and three points were still dropped (`quick-q2-superseded`). Per-point record: `run.arm.targets` = list of `{point, status met|target_not_met, shortfalls: {comparator: {reached, target}}}`, and `run.arm.met` "2 of 5 points met their target". In q3: `max_mid` and `median_low` miss on `income_tax_gains` (0.10600 against 0.17068; 0.02113 against 0.04049); `median_high` on `consumption_tax` (0.15054 against 0.16461). `median_mid` and `min_mid` met. All three misses are at the schedule chosen at `median_mid` (`tm50_k05` r100 on the household arm); no per-point schedule was tried (not ruled).
- **O1e `no_sign_change_0_15` and the NaN bug.** Under `fund_return=market` M1 responds to the generator mean (20-path probe, S_low: 0.34, 0.59, 0.76, 0.40, -0.21 at 0, 2, 5, 10, 15 percent; S_central and S_high positive throughout). `assemble._stats` returns a null for a whole `point_and_range` cell if any of its three values is non-finite, so at 0 draws (lo and hi NaN) a finite root was hidden and all three cells got the marker (q2). Fixed in `run_point`: at 0 draws the three slots are the root, then lo and hi are overwritten by `not_run_this_run`; the marker only replaces a centre that is still null. q3: S_low 11.217 and S_central 11.217 percent, S_high `no_sign_change_0_15` with ends 2.04 (0) and 4.02 (15) in `run.arm.o1e.ends_0_15`. The ends are recorded only for the cells without a root.
- **O8 nulls.** In q3, 10 of 12 O8 cells are null: reform `property` and `gains`, lines `burden` and `diff`, at S_low and S_central, and reform `property` (burden, diff) at S_high; the two S_high `gains` cells are finite. Not ruled and not diagnosed: the reform-in-place comparator (M8) returns no value for those cells; it probably comes from the same unreachable closure target (a comparator rate above 1), but I did not confirm that, nor why S_high `gains` is finite. The null makes `accept.sh` step 5 fail ("a null cell") and V5 an error in a final file. They are not the same cells as the 12 OP6 cells without a series (reported only, unchanged).
- **Identical S_low and S_central O1e roots.** Both are 11.2170410156 to ten digits in q3. The earlier 20-path probe gave different M1 curves for the three named schedules, so the schedules were different there; at the `quick` feasibility sample (50 tornado paths) they may have coincided (on the household arm only `tm50_k05` r100 was feasible and the three named schedules coincided in the first rounds). Not checked: print `named` at `quick` size, or compare the two schedules' M1 at one mean.
- **Convergence on the household arm.** The household arm ignores the level 1 path draws (`Pk = 1`: one path repeated), so the five seeds and the doubled `N1` give identical means and percentiles: C1 and C2 have zero spread and pass trivially. Only C3 (level 2) and C4 (tornado seeds) can fail.
