| These tables contain Accredited Official Statistics for the estates notified to HMRC using Inheritance Tax returns. The data is broken down by net estate size, and provides the total tax liability created, average tax paid per estate following the taxable wealth transfer, and the average effective tax rate of taxpaying estates for the tax year 2023 to 2024. The tables also provide statistics for the proportion of all UK deaths that resulted in a tax charge for a longer time period. The data are rounded to three significant figures. |
| Source: Inheritance Tax returns. Grants of representation/confirmation provided to HMRC by the three UK Courts Services (HM Courts & Tribunals Service in England & Wales, Scottish Courts & Tribunals Service in Scotland, and Northern Ireland Courts & Tribunals Service in Northern Ireland). Total UK deaths estimated using death statistics from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) for England & Wales, National Records of Scotland (NRS) for Scotland, and the Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency (NISRA) for Northern Ireland. |
