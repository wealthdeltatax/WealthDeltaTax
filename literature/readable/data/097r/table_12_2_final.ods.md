| This table contains Accredited Official Statistics on estates above the nil rate band (including the transferable nil rate band where this applies) claiming exemptions and reliefs. It shows the number of estates making claims for exemptions and reliefs, and the value of the relief or exemption provided from the chargeable estate for IHT purposes. It covers estates where details are notified to HMRC using Inheritance Tax returns. Inheritance Tax returns often do not need to be completed for estates where a person's assets are all transferred to their surviving spouse or civil partner, as no legal wealth transfer has taken place. Following a reporting requirement change for excepted estates from 1 January 2022, many non-taxpaying estates no longer need to report to HMRC. This impacts the statistics from the 2021 to 2022 tax year onwards. This table also contains the distribution of claims for agricultural and business property reliefs. The data are rounded to three significant figures. |
| Source: Inheritance Tax returns. Grants of representation/confirmation provided to HMRC by the three UK Courts Services (HM Courts & Tribunals Service in England & Wales, Scottish Courts & Tribunals Service in Scotland, and Northern Ireland Courts & Tribunals Service in Northern Ireland). |

| Note | Description |
| 1 | Some estates below the nil rate band include only exempt assets or assets qualifying for relief worth 100% of the value of the asset. Following a reporting requirement change for excepted estates from 1 January 2022, many non-taxpaying estates no longer need to report to HMRC. This impacts the statistics from the 2021 to 2022 tax year onwards. |
| 2 | Any estate benefitting from the Transferable Nil Rate Band must also be benefitting from the Nil Rate Band. |
| 3 | Net estate is defined as the combined value of assets and any lifetime gifts given in the seven years before death less the value of outstanding debts owed by the estate of the deceased person. |
| 4 | Net chargeable value is the net estate value less than the value of any reliefs set against assets. In some cases the value of reliefs and exemptions exceeds the net value of the estate. As a result, the net chargeable value can be greater than the total value of the net estate less than the total value of the reliefs and exemptions. |
| 5 | The Residence Nil Rate Band (RNRB) was introduced in the tax year 2017 to 2018 and is an additional tax-free threshold available to estates which transfer a main UK residence to direct descendants. |
| 6 | Those in the 'tax chargeable at 40%' row are recorded before any claims for relief set against tax. These numbers will be slightly higher than the number of estates with a tax liability as recorded in the 'tax payable' row, or in Table 12.3. |
| 7 | The reduced rate of Inheritance Tax only applies to estates where the date of death is on or after 6 April 2012 and where at least 10% of the qualifying estate is left to an eligible charity. |
| 8 | Reliefs set against tax includes taper relief, quick succession relief, and double taxation relief. |
| 9 | The estimates of tax payable shown here do not match to the estimates for inheritance tax receipts received by HMRC shown in other HMRC publications as they relate to tax liabilities.
