| Weighting Change |
| For April 2020-March 2022, please note survey weights are calibrated with controls applied for tenure, along with age-sex groups, and regions. This is to correct for an under-representation of renters and an over representation of households who own outright in the achieved sample interviewed during the coronavirus (COVID-19) pandemic. Population totals for the controls for this period are derived from official estimates reflecting the time of the fieldwork period. These estimates were based on data from the 2021 England and Wales and 2022 Scotland Censuses. |
